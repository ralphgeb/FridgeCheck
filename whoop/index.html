WHOOP integration

Your WHOOP data, in Halo.

A WHOOP member signs in with WHOOP from Halo's setup or Settings. Halo then reads their data read-only, works everything out on their iPhone, and keeps it there. Nothing is sent to a server, sold or shared.

Recovery

WHOOP's recovery score sits on Halo's Recovery ring, with HRV, resting heart rate, SpO₂ and skin temperature in the Health tab against the member's own normal range.

read:recovery

Sleep

WHOOP's sleep performance, stages, need and time in bed fill the Sleep tab and the sleep bank.

read:sleep

Strain and workouts

Each cycle's strain, energy and steps, and each workout's zones, heart rate and strain, drive the Effort ring, cardio load and the activity pages.

read:cycles read:workout

Profile and body

Max heart rate and weight set the heart-rate zones and calories.
