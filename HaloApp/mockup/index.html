A minimal, customisable health tracker for Fitbit. This mockup runs on six months of sample data. Scroll Today to watch the rings uncurl into bars. Tap the date for the month calendar, tap any number for its graphs (1W to 6M), drag across charts to read values, and open Settings to change the look.
