Halo

A minimal, customisable health tracker for Fitbit. This mockup runs on six months of sample data. Scroll Today to watch the rings uncurl into bars. Tap the date or pull Today down for the month calendar. Tap a ring to see what goes into it, tap any number for its graphs, step through weeks with the round arrows, drag across charts to read values. Hold a card to move it, and open Settings to change the look.
