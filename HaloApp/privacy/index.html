Halo privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Halo is a personal health tracker for iPhone. It reads your health data from the source you connect (Fitbit through Google Health, Apple Health, or Whoop) with read-only access, and works out its scores on your iPhone.

What Halo reads: sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability, recovery, workouts, activity, vitals and weight, as allowed by the permissions you grant.

Where it goes: your health data stays on your iPhone. Halo has no servers that store it, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and never sells or shares your data.

Sign-in: for Whoop, sign-in passes through a small service that swaps a sign-in code for an access token and stores nothing. Tokens are kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

Removing access: disconnect in Halo's Settings, revoke access in Google, Apple Health or Whoop, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
