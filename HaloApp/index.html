Halo

Your health made simple

Halo is an iPhone app that turns what your band already measures into three clear scores, Recovery, Effort and Sleep, each measured against your own last 30 days. It works with Fitbit (through the Google Health API), Apple Watch and Apple Health, and Whoop.

Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no cloud, no ads.
